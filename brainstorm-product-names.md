# Product Name Brainstorm: The Ankle-Breaking, Game-Changing Software

> A revolutionary all-in-one platform that found the blind spot the 50-year-old coding industry never saw coming.

---

## The 20 Names

### 1. VORTX
A vortex pulls everything into its orbit. One platform, everything collapses into it. The industry spirals around you.

### 2. BLINDSPOT
The thing hiding in plain sight for 50 years. You found it. They didn't. That's the whole pitch.

### 3. HESI
Short for hesitation move. In basketball, the hesi freezes the defender. In tech, it freezes the competition. Clean, sharp, memorable.

### 4. CROSSOVR
The crossover itself. No subtlety needed. You're breaking ankles and shipping code.

### 5. SHYFT
Paradigm shift with edge. Not "Shift" -- SHYFT. It looks different because it *is* different.

### 6. PARALLAX
A parallax is when your perspective changes and suddenly you see the real position of things. The entire industry was looking at it wrong.

### 7. JUKE
One syllable. Devastating. In football, a juke makes the defender grab air. That's what this does to legacy tools.

### 8. STEPBACK
The step-back three. Create your own space. Shoot with nobody near you. Steph Curry energy.

### 9. TORQE
Raw rotational force. Everything spins around the axis you define. Spelled different because it hits different.

### 10. SWERV
Can't be caught, can't be predicted. The tool that zigs when the whole industry zags.

### 11. PHANTOM
You can't guard what you can't see. A ghost in the system that does what nobody thought was possible.

### 12. ANKL
Brutally direct. Four letters. Everyone in basketball knows what it means. "I just got ANKL'd."

### 13. RIFTCODE
You tore a rift in the 50-year-old fabric of how software gets built. Everything pours through.

### 14. CLUTCHPOINT
The moment that decides everything. Not overtime -- the buzzer-beater. This is the tool you reach for when it all matters.

### 15. PIVOTL
The pivot foot plants, everything else moves. Fundamental and devastating at the same time.

### 16. SNAPBACK
The snapback brings you to reality. Fast, violent change of direction. The industry snaps back to where it should've been.

### 17. BLINDSIDE
They never saw it coming. 50 years of the same playbook and you came from the angle nobody was watching.

### 18. FYNT
A feint. The fake that makes the defender commit the wrong way. Spelled with a twist because it *is* the twist.

### 19. BREAKAWAY
Full court, open lane, nobody ahead of you. That's what it feels like when the tool just *works* and there's nothing in your way.

### 20. DROPSTEP
The post move that seals the defender behind you. One move, game over. Dominant and efficient.
