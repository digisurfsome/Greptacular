# Business Assessment

> **Purpose:** Systematically evaluate a prospect's business to identify EXACTLY where Claude Code adds value.
> **When to use:** During or immediately after a discovery call.
> **Output:** Custom report showing their specific opportunities, mapped to specific skills.

---

## Instructions

### Step 1: The Assessment Interview

Ask these questions (in conversation, not like a form):

**Current State:**
1. "Walk me through your typical week - what are the main tasks that eat your time?"
2. "What tools are you currently paying for?" (Get the full stack)
3. "How many people are on your team and what do they each spend most of their time on?"
4. "How many clients do you serve?"

**Pain Points:**
5. "What's the ONE thing that if it took half the time, would change your business?"
6. "What tasks do you personally hate doing but can't delegate?"
7. "Where do balls get dropped? What falls through the cracks?"

**Aspirations:**
8. "What would you do with 10 extra hours per week?"
9. "Is there anything you've wanted to offer clients but haven't because it's too time-intensive?"
10. "What's your growth bottleneck right now - leads, capacity, or delivery?"

**Tech Readiness:**
11. "Are you using AI at all right now? If so, what for?"
12. "Who on your team is most tech-savvy?"
13. "How do you feel about trying new tools?"

### Step 2: Map Their Answers to Opportunities

Take their answers and categorize into three buckets:

**REPLACE (Highest immediate value):**
Tools they're paying for that a skill can replicate.
- List each tool
- List what feature they actually use
- Map to specific skill that replaces it
- Calculate monthly savings

**IMPROVE (Highest time savings):**
Tasks they do manually that follow a repeatable pattern.
- List each task
- Estimate current time per occurrence
- Map to skill that does it faster
- Calculate hours saved per month

**ENABLE (Highest growth potential):**
Things they WANT to do but can't/don't.
- List each aspiration
- Map to skill that makes it possible
- Estimate the business impact (more clients, better retention, new revenue)

## Assessment Shortcuts by Business Type

### SEO Agency (5-20 clients)
Likely opportunities:
- Replace: AgencyAnalytics ($100-200/mo), SurferSEO ($99/mo), Link Whisper ($77/yr)
- Improve: Monthly reporting (2-4 hrs/client → 10 min), Content briefs (1-2 hrs → 5 min), Technical audits (4-8 hrs → 30 min)
- Enable: Offer content creation as add-on service, automated competitor monitoring, proactive client alerts

### Content Marketing Agency
Likely opportunities:
- Replace: Clearscope/Frase ($149/mo), Copy.ai ($49/mo)
- Improve: Brief writing, first drafts, content calendars, client reporting
- Enable: Scale to 3x output with same team, offer SEO services as add-on

### Local Service Business (Plumber, Dentist, Lawyer)
Likely opportunities:
- Replace: BrightLocal ($79/mo), review management tool ($50/mo)
- Improve: Review responses, social posts, local page creation
- Enable: Blog content (never had time before), competitor monitoring, automated GBP posting

### E-commerce
Likely opportunities:
- Replace: Product description tools ($49/mo), reporting dashboards ($99/mo)
- Improve: Product descriptions (30 min each → 2 min), category page SEO, email copy
- Enable: Personalized email sequences, automated product launch workflows

## Tips for Running the Assessment

1. **Do it live if possible.** Running this during the call (even partially) shows them the power of the system.
2. **Be honest about what WON'T work.** If something isn't a good fit, say so. Builds trust.
3. **Anchor to their pain.** Use their exact words when describing problems. "You said reporting takes 3 hours per client..."
4. **Make the math obvious.** Don't make them calculate. Show the savings explicitly.
5. **Give them a timeline.** "You'd be running this yourself in 2 weeks" is more compelling than "it's easy."
