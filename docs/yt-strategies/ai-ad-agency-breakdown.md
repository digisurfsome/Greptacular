## Critical Caveats & Considerations

1. **Terms of Service**: The creator acknowledges uncertainty about whether automated Instagram DM outreach complies with the platform's terms of service. Automated messaging on social platforms generally violates ToS and can result in account suspension.

2. **Ad Compliance**: While the AI produced accurate ad copy in this test, automotive advertising is subject to state and federal regulations (FTC, state DMV requirements, truth-in-advertising laws). Human review of ad compliance is strongly recommended before publishing.

3. **Account Security**: Although no credentials are shared with the AI, the computer-use model means the AI is operating within already-authenticated browser sessions. This carries inherent risk if the AI takes unintended actions.

4. **Scalability of Computer Use**: Browser-based automation is inherently slower and more fragile than API-based automation. Screen layouts change, pages load at variable speeds, and UI elements may shift. For production-scale operations, a hybrid approach (computer use for setup, APIs for ongoing management) would be more robust.

5. **Quality Control**: The creator reviewed the outputs at each step. For a real agency, a human quality control layer is essential -- AI-generated ads should be reviewed before going live, especially in regulated industries.

6. **Workshop Upsell**: The video serves partly as a funnel for the March 3rd paid workshop at wopacademy.com. The demonstration is genuine but is also designed to sell the educational product.
