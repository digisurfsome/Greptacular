## Scraping Strategy

1. **Weekly Product Hunt scan** — Filter AI/SaaS category, check if top launches have affiliate programs
2. **Monthly YC batch review** — New YC batches = new funded startups that will need growth = affiliate programs coming soon
3. **AppSumo monitoring** — These tools are desperate for users and almost always have affiliate programs
4. **PartnerStack browse** — This is literally a marketplace of B2B SaaS affiliate programs. Filter by new additions
5. **Set Google Alerts** for: "AI SaaS affiliate program", "AI automation partner program", "[tool name] affiliate program"
