# Cold Email for SEO Agencies

> **Replaces:** Instantly, Smartlead, Lemlist templates
> **Saves:** $30-$97/mo in tool costs (for the writing/strategy part - still need sending infrastructure)

## What This Skill Does

Generates complete cold email campaigns for SEO agencies looking to acquire new clients. Includes prospect research, email sequences, follow-ups, personalization frameworks, and response handling.

## When to Use

- Agency needs new clients
- Launching outreach to a new vertical
- Testing new offer positioning
- Building a client acquisition pipeline
- Running cold email on behalf of an SEO agency client

## Instructions

### Step 1: Gather Inputs

Ask for:
1. **Agency name and URL**
2. **Target vertical** - What types of businesses to target (dentists, lawyers, plumbers, SaaS, etc.)
3. **Target location** (if relevant) - City, state, or national
4. **Core offer** - What's the lead magnet / initial offer?
5. **Proof points** - Results, case studies, testimonials
6. **Sender info** - Who is sending (name, title, email domain)
7. **Volume goal** - How many emails per day/week
8. **Current situation** - Cold start or existing pipeline

### Step 2: Offer Positioning

The offer matters more than the email copy. Help define:

**Offer Framework:**
```
For [specific type of business] in [location/vertical],
we [specific result you deliver]
in [timeframe]
[proof/guarantee if applicable].
```

**Strong SEO Agency Offers (pick or customize):**

| Offer Type | Example | Best For |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| Free audit | "I recorded a 5-min video audit of your site" | New agencies building pipeline |
| Competitor comparison | "Your competitor [name] is outranking you for [keyword] - here's why" | Mid-market businesses |
| Quick win | "Found 3 pages on your site that could rank page 1 with minor fixes" | Businesses already investing in SEO |
| Revenue projection | "Businesses like yours in [city] are getting [X] leads/mo from SEO" | Business owners focused on ROI |
| Lost traffic alert | "Your site lost rankings for [keyword] last month - want to see why?" | Businesses with declining traffic |

### Step 3: Email Sequence

**Email 1: The Opener (Day 1)**

```
Subject: [Personalized - see framework below]

Hi [First Name],

[1 line of genuine personalization about their business]

[Problem statement - 1-2 sentences about what you noticed]

[Your offer - what you'll do for them, specific and tangible]

[Soft CTA - question, not a demand]

[Signature]
```

**Email 2: The Value Add (Day 3)**

```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi [First Name],

[Reference to first email without being pushy]

[Share a specific insight, tip, or quick win they can use whether they hire you or not]

[Restate offer differently]

[CTA]
```

**Email 3: The Social Proof (Day 7)**

```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi [First Name],

[Brief reference to previous emails]

[Case study or result: "We helped [similar business type] go from X to Y in Z months"]

[Specific metric that matters to their business type]

[CTA]
```

**Email 4: The Breakup (Day 14)**

```
Subject: Should I close your file?

Hi [First Name],

[Acknowledge they're busy]

[Restate core value prop in one sentence]

[Simple binary CTA: "Worth a conversation, or should I stop reaching out?"]
```

### Step 4: Subject Line Framework

**Rules:**
- Under 40 characters
- Lowercase (feels personal, not marketing)
- No spam trigger words (free, guaranteed, act now)
- Personalized when possible

**Templates:**
```
[first name] - quick question about [company]
[company]'s SEO vs [competitor]
noticed something on [company].com
idea for [company] re: [keyword/service]
[city] [business type] SEO question
```

### Step 5: Personalization Framework

For each prospect, find and use ONE of:
- Recent Google review they received (reference it)
- A blog post they published (comment on it)
- A specific service page on their site (relate to it)
- A local event they sponsored (mention it)
- Their Google Business Profile (note something specific)
- A keyword they're ranking on page 2 for (show the opportunity)

**Personalization line format:**
```
"Saw your [specific thing] - [genuine observation or compliment that connects to your offer]"
```

### Step 6: Response Handling

**Positive Response (interested):**
```
Great to hear from you [Name]. 

[Address their specific question/concern]

I've got a few openings this week - would [Day] at [Time] or [Day] at [Time] work for a quick 15-minute call? 

I'll walk you through exactly what I found and what the opportunity looks like for [Company].
```

**Objection - "Already have an SEO person":**
```
Totally get it [Name]. Not looking to replace anyone.

Quick question though - are you currently ranking on page 1 for [their most valuable keyword]? 

If yes, your person is doing great work. If not, might be worth a second opinion. Either way, no pressure.
```

**Objection - "Not interested":**
```
No worries at all [Name]. Appreciate you letting me know.

If things change down the road, I'm here. In the meantime, one quick tip that might help regardless: [give them a genuinely useful SEO tip specific to their site].

Best of luck with [Company].
```

**Objection - "Too expensive / no budget":**
```
Totally understand [Name]. SEO is an investment.

Quick thought - what if we started with just [smallest offer - one page optimization, one audit] so you can see the impact before committing to anything bigger? 

Most of our clients in [vertical] see [specific result] within [timeframe] from just that.
```

## Output Format

Save to: `outputs/cold-email/[campaign-name]-[date].md`

## Important Notes

This skill generates the STRATEGY and COPY. For actually sending emails at scale you still need:
- Sending infrastructure (Instantly, Smartlead, or similar)
- Email warmup
- Verified prospect lists
- Separate sending domains (don't send from your main domain)
- SPF/DKIM/DMARC configured

The value here is replacing the $97/mo template libraries and copywriting time, not the sending infrastructure itself.
