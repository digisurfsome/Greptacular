## FAQ

**Do I need to be technical?**
Can you send a text message? Then you can use this.

**How is this different from ChatGPT?**
ChatGPT answers questions and forgets you. NormieForge DOES things and remembers everything. It connects to your actual accounts, takes actual actions, and learns your actual patterns.

**Is my data safe?**
Your data runs on YOUR accounts (your bank, your calendar, your storage). We never see your financial data. Your AI works for you, not us.

**What if something breaks?**
Press the help button. AI reads your screen and fixes it. Usually takes about 5 seconds.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. But you won't want to. After a month, it knows you too well.

# DESIGN NOTES

## The Vibe
- Warm, not techy. Think Cash App meets a good friend.
- No dashboards in the hero. No screenshots of software. Just the input field.
- Colors: warm, approachable. Not startup blue. Not AI purple.
  Think: soft cream background, warm coral accent, dark text.
- Font: rounded, friendly. Not corporate. Not monospace.
- The whole page should feel like a conversation, not a product page.

## The Psychology
- Start with emotion ("what's stressing you out") not features
- The input field IS the product. It's the first interaction.
  Type something → see it work → now you're a user.
- Social proof should feel like texts from friends, not testimonials
- Pricing section should feel like "of course" not "ugh, paywall"
- The free tier exists to create the habit. The upgrade sells itself.

## The Viral Loop
1. User gets morning briefing
2. Screenshot looks beautiful and personal
3. Posts it / shows a friend: "look what my AI sent me this morning"
4. Friend: "how do I get that"
5. Friend types their one thing
6. Repeat

The briefing IS the marketing. Make it screenshot-worthy.

## Key Conversion Insight
The text input in the hero is doing two things:
1. It's capturing the user's actual problem (personalization from second one)
2. It's the onboarding. By the time they hit "Fix it" they've already
   told you what they need. No signup form. No "pick a plan."
   Just: problem → solution → hooked → then we ask for email.
