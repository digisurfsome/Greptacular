## WHAT WE DO NOT SAY IN ANY EMAIL

- "review gating" → say "automated review requests"
- "your reviews are bad" → say "a pattern we noticed"
- "we found X complaints" → say "based on our research"
- "your customers are unhappy" → say "a few reviewers mentioned"
- Never quote a review that could identify a specific customer by name/date/detail
